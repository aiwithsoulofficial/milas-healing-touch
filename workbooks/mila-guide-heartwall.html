What is the Heart Wall?

10 Minutes
  • The heart is the most electromagnetic organ in your body - its field extends up to 12 feet around you
  • The heart has its own nervous system - over 40,000 neurons - sometimes called the "heart brain"
  • The heart sends more signals to the brain than the brain sends to the heart
  • When we experience intense emotional events - heartbreak, grief, trauma, betrayal, loss - the subconscious builds a protective wall around the heart using trapped emotions
  • Layer by layer, emotion by emotion, the Heart Wall forms
Say This

"Your subconscious built your Heart Wall to protect you. It was doing its job. But the wall that once kept you safe may now be the very thing keeping you stuck - blocking love, connection, abundance, and the full experience of being alive."

Reference

Dr. Bradley Nelson discovered that approximately 93% of people have a Heart Wall. It is built by the subconscious as protection during times of emotional distress - using whatever trapped emotions are available as building material.

What is the Heart Wall Made Of?

10 Minutes
  • Each Heart Wall is completely unique - made of different trapped emotions
  • When you ask the subconscious what the Heart Wall is "made of," it gives you a metaphor - a physical material
  • The material tells you about how strong the protection is and how long it has been there

Common Materials:

  • Wood - natural, organic protection
  • Stone or brick - heavy, long-standing
  • Metal or steel - hard, impenetrable
  • Glass - fragile but sharp
  • Concrete - thick and dense
  • Ice - cold, frozen grief
Say This

"The material is a message from your subconscious. If your Heart Wall is made of steel, that tells you something different than if it's made of wood. There's no wrong answer - just information."

Interactive Moment

Ask the group: "Muscle test right now - do you have a Heart Wall? If yes, ask your subconscious what it's made of." Give them 2 minutes. Ask a few people to share.

Thickness varies too:

Some Heart Walls are paper thin. Some are 20 feet thick. Some have been there since childhood. You can ask: "How thick is my Heart Wall?" and muscle test for the answer.

The Emotion Code Chart Review

10 Minutes
Say This

"If you've done the Emotion Code Basics workshop, you already know this chart. This is a quick refresher because we're about to use it live."

How to Use the Chart:

  • Step 1: Ask "Is this trapped emotion in Column A?" - muscle test for yes or no. If no, it is in Column B.
  • Step 2: Ask "Is this emotion in an odd row?" - then narrow to the specific row (1-6).
  • Step 3: Once you have the row, test each emotion in that row until you get a "yes." That is the trapped emotion.

For the Heart Wall specifically, ask:

  • "Is the first trapped emotion in the Heart Wall in Column A?"
  • Narrow down to the row, then the specific emotion
  • Once identified, ask for details if guided: "When was this trapped?" "Was it absorbed?" "Was it inherited?"
Quick Check

Ask the group: "Does everyone have their Emotion Code Chart ready? Hold it up." Make sure everyone can see it before you proceed to the live demo.

How to Clear the Heart Wall - LIVE Demo

20 Minutes
Open With

"I'm going to walk you through the exact process I use with my clients. Watch first, then you'll do it yourself."

The 7-Step Process:

  • 1. Connect - Set your intention. Take a breath. Connect to your higher self. Ask for permission to work on the Heart Wall. This is not a rushed process - you go in with respect.
  • 2. Ask - "Do I have a Heart Wall?" Muscle test for yes or no. If yes, you are ready to begin identifying the emotions within it.
  • 3. Identify - Use the Emotion Code Chart. Ask: "Is the first trapped emotion in the Heart Wall in Column A?" Narrow it down to the row, then to the specific emotion.
  • 4. Get Details - Once you have the emotion, ask the subconscious for more information. "When was this emotion trapped?" "Was it absorbed from someone else?" "Was it inherited?" You do not need details to release it, but they can bring powerful awareness.
  • 5. Release - Swipe a magnet (or your hand) along the governing meridian - from the forehead, over the top of the head, and down the back of the neck. Three swipes for a personal emotion. Ten swipes for an inherited emotion.
  • 6. Confirm - Muscle test to confirm: "Has this emotion been released from the Heart Wall?" You should get a yes. If not, swipe again.
  • 7. Repeat - Continue identifying and releasing emotions until the subconscious says the Heart Wall is gone - or until it says you have released enough for today.

Guided Practice - Participants Clear

15 Minutes
Lead the Group Through This

Walk them through steps 1-7 in real time. Go slowly. Pause between each step so they can muscle test and work through their own Heart Wall.

Step 1 - Guide Them

"Close your eyes. Take a breath. Set your intention to connect to your higher self. Ask for permission to work on your Heart Wall today."

Step 2 - Guide Them

"Ask your body: Do I have a Heart Wall? Muscle test. If yes, let's begin."

Step 3 - Guide Them

"Ask: Is the first trapped emotion in my Heart Wall in Column A? Test. Narrow to the row. Find the emotion. Take your time."

Steps 4-7 - Guide Them

"Once you have the emotion, ask for any details your subconscious wants to share. Then take your magnet, and swipe from forehead, over the top of your head, down the back of your neck. Three times for personal, ten for inherited. Then confirm: has it been released? If yes, move to the next one."

Important

Remind them: "Your body will tell you when it has released enough for today. If your muscle test starts giving unclear answers, that is your signal to stop. Do not force it. Honor what the body is ready to do."

Processing Time

"Your body needs time to process after releasing trapped emotions. Typically 1-3 days between sessions, though it can be longer. Your subconscious will tell you when you are ready for the next round. Listen to your body. Do not rush this."

What Happens After + Self-Care

10 Minutes
Say This

"Some people feel the difference immediately. Others notice it unfolding over days and weeks. There is no single correct experience. What matters is that the wall is no longer there, and the heart can do what it was always meant to do."

What They May Experience:

  • Feeling lighter - a weight they didn't know they were carrying lifts. Chest opens. Breathing changes.
  • Emotional waves - crying, vivid dreams, waves of emotion moving through. This is normal processing.
  • More connection - relationships shift. More open, more present, more willing to let people in.
  • Fatigue - the body is doing energetic work. Rest is not laziness - it is part of the healing.
  • Abundance opens - financial shifts, career opportunities, creative breakthroughs.
  • Vivid dreams - the subconscious processes through dreams. This is normal.

Self-Care Protocol:

  • Drink plenty of water - helps the body move and process released energy
  • Rest when your body asks for it - sleep, nap, lie down
  • Be gentle with yourself - you just released something you may have carried for decades
  • Journal what comes up - memories, feelings, dreams, body sensations
  • Give yourself time to integrate - wait 1-3 days minimum before the next clearing session
  • Epsom salt bath - 20 minutes in warm water with 1-2 cups of Epsom salt

5 Minutes

Answer - Can It Come Back?

"Once the emotion is cleared, it's cleared. Emotions can get stuck again if the person does not do the work required of them to respond differently in the situation that allowed the original emotion to get stuck in the first place."

Answer - How Do I Know It's Gone?

"Muscle test: Do I still have a Heart Wall? When the answer is no, it's gone. Some people release it in one session. Others take multiple sessions over days or weeks. There is no wrong pace."
